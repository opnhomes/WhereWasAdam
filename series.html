The Series

The Gap

Helping men move from where they are today to where the Lord is calling them.

Nine reflections on where men are called to show up — no longer standing in silence.

Nine Parts

The Gap Series

I

Identity

The Question We Need to be Asking

Who are we — really? Not who society says we're supposed to be. Not the roles we fill or the titles we carry. The question of identity is the first question, and it doesn't go away until it's answered. This reflection goes to the root: who God made us to be, and what it looks like to live from that truth.

"God created mankind in his image; in the image of God he created them; male and female he created them." Genesis 1:27
Week 1 · Live Oct 11Read the reflection →
II

Interior Life

The War Is Inside

Every gap we leave in our marriages, our fatherhood, our friendships — it starts somewhere inside us. Fear. Pride. Comfort. Lust. This reflection goes to the root before anywhere else: the interior life of a man who wants to show up but keeps going silent. Where the real battle is. How to fight it.

"With all vigilance guard your heart, for in it are the sources of life." Proverbs 4:23
Week 2 · Live Oct 18Read the reflection →
III

Marriage

You're Still the Man She Married

We married her to love her — fully, sacrificially, the way Christ loved the Church. That calling didn't expire. This reflection is about what it looks like to show up for our wives in a way that costs us something, drawing on Tobit, Ephesians, and St. John Paul II's Theology of the Body.

"Husbands, love your wives, even as Christ loved the church and handed himself over for her." Ephesians 5:25
Week 3 · Goes live Oct 25
IV

Fatherhood

The Weight of the Word

There is no word in the human language more loaded with expectation than father. It carries everything — protection, presence, blessing, legacy. And for too many of us, shame. Because somewhere between the man we intended to be and the man we became, a gap opened up. And our children are living in it.

"Tend the flock of God in your midst... Do not lord it over those assigned to you, but be examples to the flock." 1 Peter 5:2–3
Week 4 · Goes live Nov 1
V

Parish & Faith

Where Are the Men?

So much of the spiritual weight of our families is carried by the women in our lives, while many of us stay quiet, distracted, or on the sidelines. Faith was never meant to be carried alone. This reflection is about what it looks like for us to lead from our knees.

"As for me and my house, we will serve the Lord." Joshua 24:15
Week 5 · Goes live Nov 8
VI

Friendship

Men Without Brothers

The loneliness epidemic among men is real. We let friendships thin out, and we call it busy. We lose the men who would have told us the truth. This reflection is about what it takes to build — or rebuild — the kind of friendship that can bear weight.

"Iron is sharpened by iron; one person sharpens another." Proverbs 27:17
Week 6 · Goes live Nov 15
VII

Workplace

The Meeting Nobody Wanted to Run

There are moments in every professional life where something wrong is happening and everyone in the room knows it — and everyone waits for someone else to speak first. This reflection is about integrity in the ordinary: what it costs to say the true thing, and what it costs not to.

"Let your 'Yes' mean 'Yes,' and your 'No' mean 'No.' Anything more is from the evil one." Matthew 5:37
Week 7 · Goes live Nov 22
Bonus

Gratitude

The One Who Turned Back

Nine lepers walked on, healed and silent. One turned back to say thank you, out loud. A short reflection for the week of Thanksgiving on gratitude spoken, not just felt — and what it costs us to stay quiet even in the good moments.

"Has none but this foreigner returned to give thanks to God?" Luke 17:18
Week 8 · Goes live Nov 29
VIII

Capstone

Where Are You?

The question this whole series opened with, asked one last time — not as judgment, but as decision. Eight weeks of reflection end here, at the same gap named back in Ezekiel — with someone finally willing to stand in it, and say so out loud.

"Where are you?" Genesis 3:9
Week 9 · Goes live Dec 6
