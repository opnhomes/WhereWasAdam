An Apostolate of Where Was Adam

Mary Magdalene
Apostolate

She stayed when others fled. We keep watch in prayer for the exploited, the buyers, and those who profit.

Why We Pray

Adam was given a garden to keep. He stood by and said nothing.

The man was placed in the garden to guard it. When the enemy came, he was standing right there. This apostolate answers that failure the way the Church always has: by showing up and keeping watch in prayer.

We pray outside strip clubs, on public property, quietly and together. We pray for the women inside, for the men who go in, and for those who profit. We come in humility, to love and to pray, not to judge.

"The LORD God took the man and put him in the garden of Eden to till it and keep it." Genesis 2:15
Our Patron

St. Mary Magdalene

Mary, called Magdalene, was a woman set free. Christ drove seven demons out of her, and she followed him from then on. When the disciples scattered on the night of his arrest, she did not run. She stood at the foot of the cross.

On Easter morning she was the first to see the risen Lord, and he sent her to tell the apostles. The Church honors her as the "apostle to the apostles." She is the patron of this work: someone who knew deliverance, stayed through the darkest hour, and carried the good news to others.

"Standing by the cross of Jesus were his mother, and his mother's sister, Mary the wife of Clopas, and Mary Magdalene." John 19:25
"Mary Magdalene went and said to the disciples, 'I have seen the Lord.'" John 20:18
Our Intentions

Who we pray for

  • The women inside: that they would know their dignity as daughters of God, be freed from fear and need, and find work that honors them.
  • The men who come: for conversion of heart, that they would see the image of God in every person and be freed from the slavery of lust.
  • Those who own and profit: that God would awaken their consciences and lead them to repentance and mercy.
  • Victims of trafficking: that God would liberate the enslaved, heal those who have been harmed, and bring to justice those who exploit them.
  • Wounded marriages and children: for husbands and wives hurt by pornography and infidelity, and for children exposed to it.
The Prayer

Order of prayer

  1. Sign of the Cross and Litany of Humility
  2. Rosary: Sorrowful Mysteries
  3. Chaplet of Divine Mercy
  4. Intentions
  5. Prayer of the Angel of Fatima
  6. Prayer to End Trafficking
  7. Memorare and Closing

The full prayers are in the prayer packet below.

Our Conduct

How we show up

  • Pray quietly, from public property, and stay together.
  • Do not argue with, photograph, or call out anyone who passes.
  • If someone approaches, listen kindly. Do not debate.
  • Keep to the order of prayer. Stay in pairs, and call the group leader about any concern.
  • If someone asks for help, offer the resource card and stay calm.

Take it with you.
